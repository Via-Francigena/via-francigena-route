About the walk

A route of purpose, community and endurance

The walk follows the classic Via Francigena pilgrimage path, tracing a journey across alpine valley towns, medieval villages, and historic Italian landscapes. It is designed as a flexible, drop-in route that can be joined for a single day or for longer sections.

Each stage brings together walking, reflection, local culture and shared endeavour. The route is not only a physical challenge but also a chance to raise awareness and support for a cause that matters deeply to the group.

Purpose

Raising funds for Charitable Cause Name

The walking challenge is being organised to raise money for Charitable Cause Name. Every contribution helps support the mission and work of the organisation, and every step taken contributes to a wider effort of generosity and practical help.
