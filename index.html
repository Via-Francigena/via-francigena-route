Welcome

The Ordinariate of Our Lady of Walsingham — Pilgrimage to Rome 2027

Fr Nicholas Leviseur, accompanied by members and friends of the Ordinariate, will walk over 1,000 km from the Swiss Alps to Rome along the ancient Via Francigena — the route first documented in AD 990 by Sigeric, 28th Archbishop of Canterbury, on his return journey from receiving his pallium.

Pilgrims are warmly invited to join for as long or as short a distance as they can manage — whether a single day, a week, or the full journey to St Peter's. Come and be part of this once-in-a-lifetime journey.

All donations raised will support PLACEHOLDER TEXT. Whatever you can give — large or small — makes a real difference.
