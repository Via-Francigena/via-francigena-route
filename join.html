AIGLE TO ROME

How to join

This walk is designed as a flexible drop-in, drop-out journey. Join for one stage, a few days, or the entire route. You choose where to begin and when to leave.

Plan ahead for weather, transport, accommodation and rest days. Carry route information, a charged phone and emergency contact details for the section you are walking.

Useful preparation

  • Wear in your walking shoes and test your pack before joining.
  • Book accommodation early in popular towns and at weekends.
  • Allow extra time for local trains, buses and transfers.
  • Carry route information, a charged phone and emergency contact details.
